OTC Pharmacy Sales Dashboard

Sales analysis using Excel formulas, pivot tables, charts and slicers.

What the workbook demonstrates

  • A structured master-data sheet with calculated fields.
  • Sales summaries by person, product, country and month.
  • Pivot tables, charts and dashboard layouts.
  • Use of formulas for monthly sales, target achievement and growth measures.

Dataset summary

The workbook contains 333 sales records across OTC products, salespeople and countries. The sample totals approximately $58.9K in sales and 3,488 boxes shipped.

What I would improve next

I would add a short data dictionary, standardize the target calculation and include a visible refresh date so another user can understand the business rules quickly.

Portfolio note: This is a user-provided workbook included as an Excel work sample.
