Application Status Portal Wireframe

A low-fidelity responsive concept designed to reduce status uncertainty, repeat inquiries and missed document requests.

Objective

Design a simple status experience that helps applicants understand where their request stands, what is required next and when they can expect an update.

Problem statement

Users often contact support because status labels are vague, requested documents are difficult to track and next steps are not visible. The concept focuses on transparency and self-service.

Key design decisions

  • Use a visible progress timeline with completed, current and upcoming stages.
  • Show outstanding actions at the top of the page rather than hiding them in messages.
  • Maintain the same information hierarchy on desktop and mobile.
  • Use plain-language status descriptions and expected response windows.

Next iteration

Validate the concept with five representative users, test whether status labels are understood and convert the strongest version into a clickable Figma prototype.

Portfolio note: This is an independently created sample. It is not based on a confidential employer system.
